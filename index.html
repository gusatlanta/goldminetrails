The land

One property. Several places to build a life.

The land rolls from an open front pasture along the road, back through hardwoods and pines, to a pond that sits below a grass dam. The walk along the top of the dam at sunrise is the best stretch on the property.

Mowed trails loop through the back meadow, along the tree line, and down to the water. A gravel drive comes in off Franklin Goldmine Road, and a barn sits at the edge of the woods. It has been kept up as a working piece of ground, not left to grow over.

Open pasture

Cut and cleared fields at the front and back of the property. Room for homesites, gardens, or animals.

The pond

A quiet pond with a grass dam you can walk across. Fishing, swimming, or just a chair at the water's edge.

Woods and trails

Hardwood and pine woods with mowed trails already cut through them. Shade in summer, privacy all year.
